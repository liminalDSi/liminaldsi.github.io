An extremely long time ago, I had my friend Marina design a character for Chapter 5 named Apple Girlington. She would've been a shopkeeper in the first area who sells you her delicious apples. It goes without saying, but she gets angry if you don't like her delicious apples…

In the end I felt like she overlapped too much with Pink, so I decided not to include her in the game. Plus, apples grew to have some other meanings in the game's story so it would've been a bit strange.

That being said, she had an amazing character design and Marina drew her many times over the years, so go check out all the drawings!!!
